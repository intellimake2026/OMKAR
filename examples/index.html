Broaching

✓ VerifiedSubtractive Manufacturing

Uses a multi-tooth cutting tool to produce internal or external profiles in a single pass.

ⓘ  Overview

Broaching is a machining process that uses a specialized multi-tooth cutting tool called a broach. Each successive tooth removes a small amount of material, allowing both roughing and finishing operations to occur in a single pass. It is commonly used to produce internal and external geometries such as keyways, splines, and complex profiles with high accuracy and excellent surface finish.

⚙  Key Characteristics

  • Single-pass operation
  • Multi-tooth cutting tool
  • Successive cutting teeth
  • Roughing and finishing integrated
  • High repeatability
  • Good surface finish
  • Close dimensional tolerances
